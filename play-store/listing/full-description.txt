SoferBooks Library is a free app for reading the classic Jewish texts: Tanakh, Mishnah, Talmud Bavli and Yerushalmi, Halacha, Midrash, Kabbalah, Chassidus, Mussar, the siddur and thousands of commentaries, in Hebrew and, where there is one, in English.

READ ANYWHERE, EVEN WITHOUT INTERNET
Download the books you want once, and read them on a plane, on Shabbos, or wherever there is no connection. Choose a whole library or only some books, and remove them again in Settings whenever you like.

EVERY PASSAGE WITH ITS CONNECTIONS
Tap any line to see its commentaries, translations and the other texts that quote it. Show a commentary such as Rashi or Onkelos right under each line, the way a printed sefer has it.

YOUR LIBRARY, YOUR CHOICE
A content filter lets you choose which books are shown, so the library holds what is right for you and your family.

MADE FOR LEARNING
- Today's learning on the home page: the parsha, Daf Yomi, Rambam, Mishnah Berurah, Tehillim and Chok LeYisrael
- Tabs: keep several sefarim open and move between them; pin the ones you learn every day
- Search the whole library, in Hebrew or English
- Three looks (white, cream and dark), adjustable text size, and fonts made for nikud and ta'amim, with the shva na and kamatz katan marked where the text has them
- Nikud by computer (from Dicta) for texts that have none, only when you turn it on
- A Sephardi siddur (Edot HaMizrach) laid out as in print, and Chok LeYisrael day by day

WHERE THE BOOKS COME FROM
The texts come from Sefaria, Dicta, Otzaria, Hebrew Wikisource, Pninim and SoferBooks' own library, each under its own free licence, shown on the book's information page.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. What you read, your tabs and your saved texts stay on your device.
